# User Research Plan: Size Uncertainty & Return Friction

## 1. Research Objectives
- Determine the psychological decision threshold when shoppers select apparel sizes.
- Quantify what percentage of returns could be eliminated with personalized sizing recommendations.
- Identify the information hierarchy shoppers look for on product detail pages.

## 2. Participant Screener & Cohorts
- **Sample Size**: 25 participants across Tier-1 and Tier-2 Indian cities.
- **Cohort A (10 participants)**: Frequent online shoppers who have made 3+ apparel returns in the last 60 days.
- **Cohort B (10 participants)**: First-time buyers of ethnic apparel on marketplaces.
- **Cohort C (5 participants)**: Shoppers who frequently abandon carts at size selection.

## 3. Interview Script & Question Flow
1. Warm-up: Tell us about the last piece of clothing you bought online.
2. Discovery: How did you decide what size to pick?
3. Critical Incident: Have you ever ordered two sizes of the same item? What led to that?
4. Prototype Walkthrough: Walk through the ThreadIQ Smart Size Predictor mockup. What is your reaction?

## 4. Synthesis & Bias Minimization
- Independent coding of interview transcripts by UX Researcher and Product Manager.
- Tagging quotes against the Customer Pain Point Tracker.\n

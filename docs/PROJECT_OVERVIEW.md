# ThreadIQ: Commerce Product Discovery, Validation & Operations Intelligence Platform

## Executive Summary
ThreadIQ is an enterprise-grade, AI-powered commerce product discovery, validation, and operations intelligence platform tailored for fashion e-commerce product management teams. ThreadIQ unifies the entire product management lifecycle: customer problem discovery, market & competitive intelligence, interactive storefront prototyping, first-party product event telemetry, rigorous statistical A/B experimentation, Agile delivery management, supply chain operational intelligence, and generative AI copilot capabilities grounded in real platform database records.

## Problem Statement
In modern fashion e-commerce:
1. **Disjointed Tooling**: Customer research lives in UserTesting, roadmaps in Jira, analytics in Mixpanel, and supply chain in ERPs. Product managers lack a single source of truth connecting customer pain points directly to feature performance and logistics impact.
2. **Sizing Uncertainty**: Across apparel, sizing discrepancies between regional and western brands drive return rates upwards of 18-24%, causing severe reverse logistics cost and customer churn.
3. **Third-Party Telemetry Fragility**: Cookie deprecation and ad-blockers break client-side third-party analytics. ThreadIQ implements a compliant first-party event pipeline.
4. **Superficial Dashboards**: Most prototypes offer decorative charts without real query backends. ThreadIQ executes real SQL calculations across 20,000 events, 2,000 orders, and 5,000 customer profiles.

## Core Capabilities
- **Customer Research Workspace**: Comprehensive research projects, structured interview captures, survey builder, configurable fashion personas, pain point tracker, and 10-stage interactive customer journey mapping.
- **Market & Competitor Intelligence**: Benchmarking matrix across 12 dimensions against peers (Ajio, Nykaa Fashion) and global leaders (Zara, H&M, Meesho).
- **Fashion Storefront Prototype**: Functional catalog of 100 apparel SKUs across 10 brands and 10 categories with size selection, Smart Size Predictor, cart, checkout simulation, and order tracking.
- **1st-Party Analytics Engine**: Funnel drop-off analytics, root-cause friction investigator, cohort retention heatmaps, and RFM customer segmentation.
- **Statistical Experimentation Lab**: Stable hash-based user variant assignment, two-proportion z-tests, p-values, 95% confidence intervals, and zero-deploy feature flags.
- **Program & Agile Delivery**: Internal 7-column Kanban board, user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria, sprint planning, and burndown charts.
- **Supply Chain Intelligence**: Inbound catalogue completeness scoring, supplier lead times, inventory aging, out-of-stock buffers, and reverse logistics return telemetry.
- **AI Product Copilot**: Powered by Google Gemini API with transparent deterministic domain intelligence fallback.
- **BI & Tableau Support**: Star-schema analytical tables (Fact_Product_Events, Fact_Orders, Dim_Products, Dim_Customers) with one-click multi-sheet Excel (.xlsx) exports.\n

# Product Requirements Document (PRD): ThreadIQ Platform

## 1. Document Control
- **Document Title**: ThreadIQ Commerce Product Intelligence Platform
- **Version**: 1.0.0 Production Baseline
- **Document Owner**: Aarav Sharma (Lead Product Manager)
- **Target Launch**: Q1 2026

## 2. Business Objective & Impact
Empower cross-functional commerce squads (PM, Design, Engineering, QA, Ops) to:
- Identify and validate critical customer shopping friction before writing code.
- Reduce apparel sizing-related return rates by at least 15% through predictive size guidance.
- Eliminate dependency on costly third-party analytics tools (Mixpanel, Amplitude) via native first-party event capture.
- Automate release management, SOP governance, and cross-functional launch readiness checks.

## 3. User Personas & Permissions
1. **Product Manager**: Roadmapping, RICE prioritization, PRD authoring, release sign-offs.
2. **Product Analyst**: Funnel conversion audits, cohort analysis, statistical A/B test evaluation.
3. **UX Researcher**: Customer interview logging, usability studies, journey emotion mapping.
4. **Product Designer**: Design token specifications, component inventories, prototype flows.
5. **Software Engineer**: Story point execution, telemetry instrumentation, feature flag controls.
6. **QA Engineer**: Acceptance criteria verification, regression test sign-off.
7. **Operations Lead**: Catalogue quality review, reverse logistics SLA tracking, supplier monitoring.

## 4. Functional Scope
- **Module A**: Customer Research (Interviews, Surveys, Personas, Pain Points, Journey Map).
- **Module B**: Market Intelligence (Competitor matrix, market opportunities).
- **Module C**: Fashion Storefront Prototype (Catalog, PDP, Cart, Checkout, Order Tracking).
- **Module D**: First-Party Product Analytics (Funnel, Root-Cause, Cohorts, RFM).
- **Module E**: Experimentation Lab (Two-proportion z-tests, Feature flags, Usability tasks).
- **Module F**: Operations Intelligence (Inbound catalogue score, Inventory aging, SLA metrics).
- **Module G**: AI Product Copilot (16 automated PM workflows grounded in platform DB).
- **Module H**: Delivery Management (Kanban, Sprints, Burndown, RACI, Launch Checklist).
- **Module I**: Reports & Exports (CSV, multi-sheet XLSX, Tableau star-schema endpoints).\n

# Supply Chain & Operational Logistics Metrics

## Inbound Metrics
- **Catalogue Completeness**: Percentage of 12 mandatory attributes filled. Target $\ge 95\%$.
- **Supplier Lead Time**: Days from Purchase Order placement to warehouse receipt. Target $\le 7$ days.

## Outbound Metrics
- **Out-of-Stock Rate**: SKUs with zero inventory. Target $\le 3.5\%$.
- **Fulfilment Cycle Time**: Hours from order confirmation to carrier handover. Target $\le 36$ hours.
- **On-Time Delivery SLA**: Shipments delivered within promised window. Target $\ge 94\%$.\n
